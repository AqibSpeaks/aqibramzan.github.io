What Does a Media Buyer Do?

A media buyer is responsible for managing paid advertising campaigns across platforms such as Meta, Google, TikTok and other digital advertising networks.

Media buying involves much more than launching advertisements. A strong media buying process includes audience research, campaign structure, budget allocation, creative testing, performance analysis and continuous optimization.

My Approach to Media Buying

My approach focuses on building a repeatable system rather than relying on a single successful campaign.

1. Understand the Business

Before changing campaigns, I look at the economics of the business, including the offer, average order value, margins, customer acquisition cost and conversion funnel.

2. Build a Testing Framework

Creative, audiences, offers and landing pages all need structured testing. The goal is to understand which variables actually influence performance.

3. Measure the Right Metrics

Depending on the business model, I monitor metrics such as CPA, CPC, CTR, conversion rate, ROAS, MER, AOV and customer acquisition cost.

4. Scale What Works

Scaling paid advertising requires balancing additional spend against efficiency. Increasing a budget is only useful when the underlying economics remain sustainable.

Performance Marketing for DTC & E-commerce

For DTC and e-commerce businesses, paid advertising cannot be viewed in isolation. Campaign performance is connected to creative quality, landing pages, conversion rate, average order value, customer retention and overall unit economics.

This is why I approach media buying as part of a broader performance marketing system rather than simply managing advertisements inside an ad platform.
